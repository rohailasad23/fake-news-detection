Methodology

How the system works

From raw news text to a verified, tamper-proof record — seven steps.

  1. 01

    Data collection

    Real & fake news from the FakeNewsNet and LIAR datasets.

  2. 02

    Preprocessing

    NLTK cleaning, tokenization, stop-word removal & normalization.

  3. 03

    Feature extraction

    Text becomes numbers with TF-IDF and word embeddings.

  4. 04

    Model training

    RoBERTa, CNN and LSTM, plus LR, Random Forest & SVM.

  5. 05

    Fake news detection

    The trained model classifies content as Real or Fake.

  6. 06

    Blockchain traceability

    Source stored on Ethereum with a unique, unalterable hash.

  7. 07

    User interface

    Instant classification with source verification status.
